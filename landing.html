Game Systems

9 Interconnected Life Systems

Every system feeds into every other. Your career affects your net worth. Your crime record affects your career. Your health affects everything. Nothing exists in isolation.

Career & Education

50+ jobs across blue-collar, white-collar, and executive tracks. Earn degrees to unlock higher tiers. Get promoted, get fired, or quit to chase bigger dreams. Your energy and smarts directly determine your trajectory.

Wealth Engines

Build passive income through real estate, franchises, bonds, and dividend stocks. Create wealth that generates money while you sleep — or watch it evaporate in a market crash.

The Underworld

Petty theft, burglaries, crypto scams, and bank heists. Build street rep to unlock bigger scores — but higher heat means longer prison sentences. An interactive prison system awaits if you get caught.

High Stakes Casino

Professional bankroll management meets pure gambling chaos. Track win rates, streaks, and lifetime P&L. The house always has an edge — but the rush of a 10x payout hits different when it's your life savings.

Global Markets

Trade stocks, crypto, commodities, and bonds with real market dynamics. Bull runs, crashes, bubbles — all procedurally generated with realistic economic cycles. Time the market or let it time you.

Political Career

Run for City Council, work your way up to Governor, and campaign for Senator. Build influence through rallies and donations. Pass laws, give speeches, or embezzle funds at your own risk.

Banking & Debt

Open savings accounts, take personal loans, and manage credit cards — with realistic interest rates, credit scores, and debt traps. Leverage is power until it becomes a prison.

Relationships & Family

Date, marry (with prenuptial agreements), have children, adopt pets. Relationships affect happiness, unlock bonuses, and cost money. Divorce can wipe out half your net worth.

Startups & Business

Launch tech startups with $5K seed capital. Hire employees. Scale or fail. Side hustles, creator channels, and high-ticket contracts provide alternative income streams.

Getting Started

How Works

No tutorials, no hand-holding. You start at 18 with nothing but time and ambition. Here's the framework.

Start at 18

You begin with $500, no education, and no connections. The clock starts ticking. Every month costs energy, health, and sanity.

Build Income

Get a job, start a hustle, launch a business, or turn to crime. Stack multiple income streams. The grind is real — burnout is realer.

Invest & Grow

Park cash in assets that generate returns. Real estate, markets, franchises, bonds. Compound growth is the key — if you survive long enough to see it.

Survive or Retire

Your death risk increases with age, stress, and health. Retire safely with enough wealth — or push too hard and lose everything in one bad month.

Is really free?
Yes, 100% free. No in-app purchases, no premium currency, no account required. The entire game runs in your browser. We built this because we love games, not because we want your wallet.
Do I need to download anything?
No. runs entirely in your browser — Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, or any modern browser. It works on desktop, tablet, and phone. Just click "Play" and you're in.
Is my progress saved?
Yes. You can manually save at any time. As long as you don't clear your browser data, your progress is safe. We recommend saving frequently — life in is unpredictable.
How long is a typical game?
A full life from 18 to death/retirement typically takes 30-90 minutes depending on how cautiously you play and how much you use the burst feature. But most players start a "quick session" and end up playing for hours. The game is highly replayable — every run is completely different.
