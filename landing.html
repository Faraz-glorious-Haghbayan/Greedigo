Global Markets

Health & Wellness

Gym memberships, therapy, medical bills. Poor health tanks productivity and spikes death risk. Burnout is the silent killer that sneaks up on every grinder.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before diving in.

Is Greedigo really free?
Yes — 100% free. No in-app purchases, no premium currency, no account required. Greedigo runs entirely in your browser. Built out of passion for deep game design, not to extract money from players.
Do I need to download anything?
No download, no install. Greedigo runs entirely in your browser — Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge. Works on desktop, tablet, and phone. Click Play and you're in.
Is my progress saved?
Yes. You can manually save at any time. Greedigo also supports export/import saves so you never lose progress. As long as you don't clear browser storage, your life is safe. Save frequently — bad months hit fast.
How long is a typical run?
A full life from age 18 to death or retirement typically takes 30–90 minutes, depending on how cautiously you play. Most players start for "10 minutes" and end up playing for hours. Greedigo is highly replayable — each run is completely different.
What makes Greedigo different from BitLife?
Greedigo focuses on financial and systemic realism. We simulate actual economics — inflation, interest rates, market cycles, debt spirals. BitLife is a tap-based story generator. Greedigo is a strategy game disguised as a life simulator. Every system interconnects.
Can I play on my phone?
Absolutely. Greedigo is fully responsive. Collapsible stats panel, slide-out navigation, touch-friendly controls. Optimized for phones, tablets, and desktops equally.
What happens when you die?
Death is permanent — Greedigo uses permadeath. A detailed death screen shows your cause of death, peak net worth, age, and key life stats. Your past lives are stored in the Past Lives tab so you can review every run.
Is there multiplayer?
Not currently. Greedigo is a single-player experience focused on deep systems. We may explore competitive leaderboards in the future.
